# 📖 Agile Extension to the BABOK® Guide (v2) — Interactive Dashboard

An interactive HTML dashboard exploring the key concepts, frameworks and techniques from the *Agile Extension to the BABOK® Guide*, Version 2 (IIBA & Agile Alliance, 2017).

**What's inside**

- The agile mindset, the Manifesto re-read for analysis, and the BACCM™
- The seven principles of agile business analysis — and how each applies at every horizon
- A three-horizon explorer (Strategy · Initiative · Delivery) with time frames and feedback flows
- Predictive vs iterative vs adaptive planning, side by side
- A filterable library of all 24 techniques (by horizon and context), including the Kano grid, INVEST, Gherkin, Real Options and more
- A condensed BABOK® knowledge-area × horizon mapping (Appendix B)
